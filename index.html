Your privacy, in plain words

MedScan is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1

Summary

There is no account and no sign-in. Your saved medicine details stay on your device. Photos are read on your device unless you turn on smart scan, which sends only the photo you scan to an online text-reading service (OCR.space). MedScan shows ads from Google AdMob; see section 8.

2

What the app handles

Photos of medicine packaging you take or choose, the text read from them, the details you type or confirm (name, strength, batch, manufacturer, dates), and your settings such as theme and language.

3

How it is used

Text is read on your device. The details are kept so you can see your medicines and be reminded before the printed expiry dates. They are not used for anything else.

4

Text recognition

Text on a photo is read on your device unless you turn on smart scan. With smart scan, the photo you scan is sent to the online text-reading service OCR.space, only to read the text on it. To show general information about a medicine, only its name (no photo, no personal details) is sent to public health information services: U.S. FDA (openFDA), the U.S. National Library of Medicine (RxNorm, MedlinePlus, DailyMed) and Wikipedia. Results are kept on your device for offline use.

5

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a medicine removes its record and its image. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

6

Security

Your data sits in the app's private storage and is protected by your device's own security, such as the screen lock and device encryption. Anyone who can unlock your phone could open the app, so keep your device locked.

7

Permissions

Camera: only to take a photo when you ask. Photos and files: only to open an image you choose. Notifications: only to remind you about expiry dates. You can turn any of these off in your device settings.

8

Network use

The app does not send your medicine records anywhere. It shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar device and usage information, and where the law requires it you are asked for your choice first. Ads are never based on your saved medicines, and you can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. The app may also download its typeface from Google Fonts the first time it runs; your device's network address is visible to those services.

9

Sharing and backups

MedScan does not sell or share your medicine records. Ads are provided by Google AdMob under Google's own privacy policy. If your phone's own backup feature is on, the operating system may include the app's data in your device backup, under your own account and settings.

10

Children

The app is meant for adults managing medicines. A parent or guardian should manage the app for a child.

11

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new text will appear here.

Questions about this policy?

Open an issue at github.com/hegdev2026/medscan-privacy/issues and we will get back to you.
